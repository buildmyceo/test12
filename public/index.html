Culinary Leadership

Executive Chef Eleanor Vance

Trained across Lyon and the northern California wine country, Chef Eleanor Vance founded L'AURA with a singular commitment: that fine dining should be vibrant, generous, and unpretentious.

"A truly unforgettable meal doesn't come from complicating nature — it comes from listening to the morning harvest and treating every single herb with reverence."

— Eleanor Vance, Executive Head Chef
